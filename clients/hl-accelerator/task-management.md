## How tasks get on the board

Captured automatically. No need to add them by hand.

- **Email:** send to ai@cecetan.me with the subject line "New Task"
- **Discord:** tasks mentioned in City Hall, the brgy-halls and the Street threads
- **Meetings:** action items from Granola meeting notes
- **Dashboards:** HLA Graphics and HLA Videos
- **Request forms:** for design and video work (below)

## Managing my tasks

- Open the board and filter by my name.
- Statuses (board changes sync to HighLevel):
  - **New / Planned** — assigned, not started
  - **In-progress** — working on it
  - **Approval** — done, waiting for review
  - **Stuck** — blocked (add a note saying why)
  - **Completed** — finished and approved
- **Days left** column: negative = overdue.

## Ground rules

- The board is the source of truth. If it's not on the board, it isn't a task.
- No form, no creative request. Chat and DM requests aren't tracked.
- Different deadline or owner? Change it on the board or ask Gail.
- Gail reviews the board every Tuesday. It's compared against KPIs in performance reviews, so keep statuses current.
