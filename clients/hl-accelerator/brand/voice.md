# Brand voice — HL Accelerator

Pulled from hlaccelerator.com and ghl4beginners.com on 2026-11-03. Replace or extend this with an official brand guide if the client provides one.

## Tone

- Encouraging, direct and community-first ("HLA Fam")
- Mixes English with Filipino. Taglish is on-brand.
- Practical with no fluff. Positions coaches as generous people who don't gatekeep.
- Speaks to non-techy people who want premium clients and a future-proof career

## Taglines and headlines in use

- "You Don't Have to Be 'Techy' to Become a Premium Freelancer."
- "Learn Automation. Attract Premium Clients."
- "Build a Future-Proof Career with HL Accelerator."
- "Build a Stable, High-Paying, AI-Ready Career Through AI & Automation in Just 30 Days" (GHL4Beginners)

## Recurring themes

Premium freelancer, AI-ready, structured roadmap, and a supportive community (Facebook group, Discord, weekly sessions). There is also a playful community-identity language around "barangays".
