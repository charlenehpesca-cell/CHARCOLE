# Bleuciel — Process Map Walkthrough Script

For presenting the [process map](https://claude.ai/artifact/8ovBJorLrPgsLKWYnwuvbU) to Ken, Aimee, Vince and Kath. About 15–20 minutes plus Q&A. Scroll section by section. Lines in _italics_ are cues, not things to say.

---

## Opening (1 min)

"Thank you all for sending back the kickoff questionnaire. It helped a lot. Today I'll walk you through exactly how your new lead system will work, from the moment someone messages your page to the moment the deal closes.

There are three things I'd like from you today. First, check that this matches how you actually sell. Second, flag anything that feels off. Third, help us close a few open items at the end, so we can keep building toward the November 30 finish."

## 1. Overview (3 min)

_Show the big diagram. Point left to right._

"This is the whole system on one page. Read it left to right.

Leads come in two ways: from your Facebook and Instagram ads, which your team keeps running, and from people messaging your pages directly.

Ad traffic goes to a new landing page with two offers: book a free showroom visit, or book a free online consultation. Messages get an automatic first reply within minutes, even at night.

Either way, the person is saved in GoHighLevel as a contact and a deal. Nobody gets lost in the inbox anymore.

Then the system asks one question: is this a hot lead? If yes, meaning they asked for a price, a quote, a visit or a person, Vince or Kath gets notified right away and takes over.

If they go quiet, they get a short series of friendly follow-up messages until they reply or book.

When someone books, they get reminders so they actually show up. If they miss it, we invite them to rebook. And after the demo, the deal moves through Quotation, Down Payment, and Closed."

_Pause._ "Any questions on the big picture before we go into the details?"

## 2. Entry points by brand (2 min)

"You told us there are five brands: Ricoma, Sinsim, JACK, BRUCE and Bullmer. They'll all live in one GoHighLevel account, but every contact is tagged by brand.

That tag matters. It makes sure each lead gets the right brand name, the right showroom address, and the right sender email. Ricoma leads go to Batangas Street. The other four go to Zobel Roxas.

Every form will also have a privacy notice and a consent checkbox under the Data Privacy Act."

_Ask:_ "Is this table correct for all the pages and accounts?"

## 3. Auto-reply flow (3 min)

"This is what happens when someone messages your page.

The bot greets them in a friendly Taglish and English tone, then shows a few buttons: price, showroom visit, online consult, talk to a person, which machine is right for me, and other questions.

The important part is this: the bot only says what you approved in your FAQs. If they ask how much, it never gives a price. It says your team will prepare a quotation based on their needs, asks a couple of quick questions, and hands them to a person.

For Sinsim, JACK, BRUCE and Bullmer, it asks which machine they want and what they'll use it for, then passes them straight to a sales rep.

Repair or technical questions go to your service team, not sales."

_Ask:_ "Who should we route service questions to?" and "Outside 10 to 3, what should the bot say? For example, 'Our team will reply at 10am.'"

## 4. Hot lead handover (2 min)

"A hot lead is exactly what you defined: anyone asking for a price, a quotation, a showroom visit, an online consultation, or a person.

When that happens, the system assigns the lead to Vince or Kath by taking turns, then sends a notification to the GHL app and an SMS. The follow-up messages pause, so the lead doesn't get automated texts while talking to a real person.

We're also proposing a safety net: if a hot lead isn't answered within about 15 minutes during business hours, the other rep and Ken get an alert."

_Ask:_ "Do you want that safety net? And should Vince and Kath be notified for every new lead, or only hot leads and bookings?"

## 5. Booking and reminders (3 min)

"This replaces Calendly. There are two calendars: a 30-minute showroom demo and a 30-minute online consultation, Monday to Saturday, 10am to 3pm, with a 30-minute gap between appointments. Bookings rotate between Vince and Kath.

You mentioned no-shows are a problem, so here's the reminder flow you asked for. They get a confirmation right after booking. The day before, we ask them to reply YES to confirm. If they don't reply, the rep gets a heads-up to call. Two hours before, they get the map, or the video link if it's online.

If they miss it, the deal moves to No-Show and they get a friendly 'we missed you' message with a link to rebook. We also offer an online consult in case coming in is the problem."

## 6. Follow-up drip (2 min)

"For leads who stop replying, we send six short messages over about two weeks, mixing SMS and email. They come from Vince or Kath by name, friendly and honest, with no hard selling, just like you asked.

The topics use what you already gave us: the showroom invite, no experience needed, the 3-day training, lifetime support, and nationwide setup.

The moment they reply or book, the messages stop. We'll draft all of these, and you only need to review and approve."

_Ask:_ "Does a two-week sequence feel right, or too much?"

## 7. Pipeline (2 min)

"These are the stages you confirmed, including the No-Show stage you added and Closed split into Won and Lost.

Most moves happen automatically: new lead, contacted, hot lead, booked, no-show. Two are manual, because only your team knows when they happen: sending a quotation and receiving a down payment.

When a deal is marked Lost, the rep picks a reason. Over time, that tells you why deals are lost.

Every deal also carries the fields you asked for: brand, machine, lead source, assigned rep, location, timeline to buy, and so on."

## 8. monday.com import (1 min)

"Your 142 active deals will come over so the team doesn't start from zero. We'll test with about 10 first and check them with Ken, then import the rest. Imported deals won't trigger any automated messages."

_Ask:_ "When can we get the export, and which statuses should we leave out?"

## 9. Guardrails (1 min)

"To be clear about what the automation will never do: quote a price or discount, promise delivery or stock, guarantee earnings, approve payment terms, or give technical advice. Those always go to a person."

## 10. Open items and close (3 min)

"Last, here are the items we still need from you to stay on schedule."

_Go through the list. Priorities:_
1. "One final approver. You listed both Ken and Aimee. Who has the final sign-off?"
2. "The monday.com export."
3. "Logos, brand colors and fonts for the five brands."
4. "Business Manager access for the pages and Instagram accounts."
5. "Your existing SMS number and the exact sender emails."

"Next, we'll start drafting the reply flows, landing page and follow-up messages. You'll get them for review with Batch 2, and we'll ask for your feedback within five business days.

Thank you, everyone. What questions do you have?"

---

## Quick answers if asked

- **"Can the bot give prices?"** No. That was your guardrail. It always hands pricing to a person.
- **"What does GHL cost?"** GHL subscription, SMS usage, phone numbers and email sending are billed directly to you by the platforms. They aren't part of our fee.
- **"Will we still run the ads?"** Yes. Your team keeps managing ads. We connect them to the landing page and track which ads bring leads.
- **"What if someone messages in Tagalog?"** The replies are written in Taglish and English, and a person takes over for the real conversation.
