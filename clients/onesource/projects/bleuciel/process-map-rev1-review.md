## What changed on the sales side (pages 1–2)

- Stages now: New Lead → (Hot lead?) → Discovery Call → Visit booked → Demo held → Discuss / Send Agreement → **Client Purchased** (down payment) or **Not Interested** (only when client says no). Quotation, Down Payment, Closed Won/Lost dropped.
- Landing page offers: showroom visit or **discovery call**; bookings skip "Hot lead?".
- More lead sources tagged: expos (QR), Demo Days, seminars, website forms, Google ads, walk-ins.
- Drip per scenario: new lead D1/3/7/14/21; hot lead quiet D3 rep msg + D7/14/21; no-show same day/D2/D7; quiet after demo D1/4/10/20; no down payment D3 rep + D7/14/21; cancel = same-day rebook.
- After drips: 30-day callback request (one Viber/SMS) → long-term nurture (monthly email, rep check-in every 90 days; newsletter only after 12 months).
- "Not yet" = pause until client's date, then resume. Same rep always; reopen with same rep.
- Respectful-contact rule: max 2 follow-ups/week in week 1, 1/week after; send 9am–6pm Mon–Sat; calls only if agreed.
- Not-a-lead tag (job seekers, suppliers, spam); service requests routed out of sales.
- Reporting to Management + Marketing: source/campaign, brand, owner, stage, next step, machine model, units, why bought, loss reason; monthly dashboard.
