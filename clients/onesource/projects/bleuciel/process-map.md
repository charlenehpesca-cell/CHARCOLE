## Flow

Ads → landing page / FB+IG DMs → auto-reply → contact + deal in GHL (New Lead, tagged by brand + source) → hot lead? → **yes:** round-robin to Vince/Kath (app + SMS) → booking → reminders → demo → Quotation → Down Payment → Closed Won/Lost. **No reply:** ~14-day email/SMS drip, stops on reply or booking. **No-show:** "we missed you" + rebook link, online consult offered.

## Key setup decisions (from kickoff answers)

- 5 brands in one sub-account: Ricoma, Sinsim, JACK, BRUCE, Bullmer (Bullmer uses main Bleuciel FB page, no IG)
- Showrooms: Ricoma at 1278 Batangas St., Makati; others at 5865 Zobel Roxas St., Makati
- Calendars: showroom demo 30 min, online consult 30 min, Mon–Sat 10am–3pm, 30-min buffer, no daily limit, round-robin
- Reminders: confirmation → day-before (reply YES) → 2 hours before with map → no-show rebook
- Hot lead = asks price, quotation, showroom visit, online consult, or to talk to a person
- Pipeline: New Lead → Contacted → Hot Lead → Discovery Call → Showroom Demo → No-Show → Quotation → Down Payment → Closed Won / Closed Lost
- Drip: OneSource drafts; sent from Vince or Kath under brand name; friendly Taglish/English, no hard selling
- Senders: Ricoma @ricomaembroidery.com.ph; others @bleucielautomations.com; existing SMS number
- Guardrails: never quote prices/discounts, promise delivery/stock, guarantee income, approve payment terms, or give technical advice
- Data Privacy Act 2012 notice + consent on forms
- Updates via WhatsApp with Ken and Aimee; target completion Nov 30
