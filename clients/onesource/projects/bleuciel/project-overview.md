## What we're building

Facebook ads (run in-house by Bleuciel) → new landing page in their own GHL account → organized CRM pipeline, plus email/SMS follow-up, Messenger auto-response, and showroom booking that replaces Calendly.

## Objectives

- Reply to every new inquiry instantly.
- Log every lead in one CRM pipeline.
- Recover cold/non-responsive leads with automated email/SMS.
- Self-serve showroom and online demo booking with reminders to cut no-shows.
- One place for admins to work leads, with instant new-lead notifications.
- Migrate 142 active deals from monday.com into GHL.
- Hand over a company-owned GHL account and a trained team.

## Scope

**1. GHL sub-account**
- New sub-account registered under Bleuciel Automations Inc.
- 3 users: Ken Ty (admin), Vince, Kath.
- Connect Ricoma FB page (facebook.com/ricomaembroideryphilippines) + Instagram.
- Phone number(s), SMS config, email sender identity.

**2. Landing page**
- Conversion page in GHL. Offer: "Book a Showroom Visit (Makati)" or "Free Online Consultation".
- Forms wired to GHL calendar + CRM; mobile-responsive; thank-you/confirmation page.
- Lead source tracking per ad/campaign (ads stay in-house).

**3. CRM pipeline + import**
- Stages: New Inquiry → Replied → Hot Lead (asks price / showroom / quote) → Discovery Call → Showroom Demo → Quotation → Down Payment → Closed.
- Custom fields: machine interest, budget, showroom vs. online, other lead details.
- Import 142 active deals from monday.com with stages mapped.

**4. Email & SMS for non-responders**
- Multi-day drip: reminder, value (showroom invite, financing/warranty), direct CTA.
- Content drafted for client approval before activation.
- Stop rule: drip ends the moment a lead replies or books → handed to team.

**5. Messenger auto-response + notifications**
- FB + IG auto first reply within minutes, 24/7.
- Flow captures name, contact details, intent (price / showroom / quote) → logged in CRM.
- Instant notification to Vince and Kath via GHL mobile app + SMS.
- Handover to a human for the live conversation.

**6. Booking + reminders**
- Makati showroom demo (30–45 min) and online video consult (15–30 min).
- Mon–Sat, 10:00 AM–3:00 PM.
- Retire Calendly; bookings land in the pipeline.
- Confirmation + day-before and same-day reminders; reschedule/cancel links; calendar sync for Vince and Kath.

**7. Testing, training, handover**
- End-to-end QA: ad → page → CRM, Messenger, drips, notifications, booking, reminders.
- Live walkthrough/training for Ken, Vince, Kath.
- Day-to-day operating doc/SOP.
