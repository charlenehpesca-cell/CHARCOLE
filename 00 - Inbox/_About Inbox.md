# Inbox

Capture first, sort later. New notes land here by default.

## How to use it
- Dump quick thoughts, links, ideas and to-dos here without worrying where they go.
- Once or twice a week, empty it: move each note to **Projects**, **Areas**, **Resources** or **Zettelkasten**, or delete it.
- Goal: this folder is close to empty after every review.
