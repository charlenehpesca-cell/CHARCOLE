# Voice Principles

How I sound in my own messages (emails, DMs, posts, proposals). Client copy uses the client's brand voice instead.

> Starter version. To personalize: paste five of your own emails or messages into Claude and ask it to update this file.

## Core traits

- **Clear:** one idea per sentence; plain words over jargon.
- **Warm:** friendly and human, never stiff or corporate.
- **Direct:** say the point first, then the context.
- **Confident:** no over-apologizing or hedging.

## Do

- Open with the point or the ask.
- Use short paragraphs and simple sentences.
- End with a clear next step.

## Don't

- Don't use filler openers ("I hope this email finds you well").
- Don't stack exclamation marks or hype words.
- Don't pad with unnecessary qualifiers.
