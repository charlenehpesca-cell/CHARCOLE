# Zettelkasten

Permanent insights. One idea per note, written in your own words.

## Rules
- **One idea per note.** The title states the idea as a sentence (e.g. "Clients buy outcomes, not hours").
- Write it so it makes sense on its own, months from now.
- Link every new note to at least one existing note, and say why they connect.
- Note where the idea came from in `source`.
